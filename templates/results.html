What this does and does not show. The model recognises the recorded state from IR, displacement and red channels. It cannot separate ICP from other things that change with posture and straining (head angle, blood pressure, venous return): the IR and red channels alone each reach about 0.91, so systemic effects are part of the signal. Manoeuvre order was fixed, there are no patients in the hardware data, and everything is one site and one device.

2. CHARIS-only model (public patient data)

XGBoost trained on 13 patients with an invasive ICP probe; windows are abnormal when ICP was 20 mmHg or higher for more than 60% of the window. Tested leave-one-patient-out.

Why two numbers. The original features included the average pressure level, which also defines the label. With that removed the AUC is about 0.69, and XGBoost is not clearly better than simpler models.

3. Does the CHARIS model transfer to the hardware?

No. Valsalva-vs-supine AUC on hardware windows after each alignment attempt (0.5 is chance). Two of five features change in opposite directions between the datasets, which rescaling cannot fix.
